📱 Android

Install a real app icon on your home screen.

Download for Android (.apk)

Your phone will warn about "unknown sources" since this isn't from the Play Store — that's expected, tap Install anyway.

🍎 iPhone / iPad

Apple doesn't allow installing apps outside the App Store without a developer account, so instead you add the app to your home screen from Safari — it behaves just like an installed app, full screen, no browser bar.

Open in Safari
  1. Tap the Share icon (square with an arrow) at the bottom of Safari
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  3. Tap Add — the app icon now lives on your home screen

Both the Android app and the home-screen version always show whatever is currently live on this site — nothing to redownload when the app is updated.
